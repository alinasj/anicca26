Why aTask

Simple on purpose

Plain task titles, optional tags, and no projects inside projects. Everything you need to run a home together, and nothing you don't.

Real-time sync

Changes show up on everyone's phone right away.

Everything in one place

The Tasks view shows open items across all your lists, with search and filters.

Plan your week

Month, week and day views. Drag tasks to reschedule them.

Color tags

Tag tasks by the categories you actually care about.

Invite anyone

Share a list by email or with an invite link. Everyone can add and complete tasks.

Home screen widgets

See your list or calendar at a glance on iPhone and Android.
